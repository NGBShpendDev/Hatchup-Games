Some artifacts only activate when users maintain real-world fitness consistency.

Examples:
- cardio streak activates Speed Core
- hydration streak activates Recovery Crystal
- strength streak activates Titan Relic
- sleep streak activates Energy Rune

- buffs decrease
- elite abilities deactivate

This creates strong motivation for real-world consistency.
