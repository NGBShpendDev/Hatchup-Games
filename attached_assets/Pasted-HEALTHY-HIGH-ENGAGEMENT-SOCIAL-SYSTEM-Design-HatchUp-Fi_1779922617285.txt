HEALTHY HIGH-ENGAGEMENT SOCIAL SYSTEM:

Design HatchUp Fitness Pals to become a daily destination users genuinely WANT to open because it improves:
- their fitness
- their confidence
- their friendships
- their progression
- their emotional connection to their creature pals

The platform should combine:
- social networking
- creature collecting
- fitness progression
- live events
- community motivation
- real-world exploration

The experience should feel more rewarding and meaningful than passive scrolling apps.

- achievement

- emotional attachment
