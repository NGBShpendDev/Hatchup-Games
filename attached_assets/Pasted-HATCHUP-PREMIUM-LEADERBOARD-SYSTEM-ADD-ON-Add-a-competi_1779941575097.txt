HATCHUP PREMIUM LEADERBOARD SYSTEM ADD-ON

Add a competitive leaderboard system to HatchUp Fitness Pals that motivates users through local rankings while offering premium global competition through the HatchUp Power Pack subscription.

The leaderboard system should feel:
- competitive
- motivating
- social
- rewarding
- prestigious
- addictive

====================================
FREE USER LEADERBOARDS
====================================

Free users can access:
- neighborhood rankings
- local city rankings
- nearby friend rankings
- local gym/community rankings

Users can compete locally for:
- steps
- workouts completed
- streaks
- calories burned
- verified reps
- challenge wins
- creature evolution progress

This keeps the app exciting and community-focused even for free users.

- State leaderboards

- elite athletes
