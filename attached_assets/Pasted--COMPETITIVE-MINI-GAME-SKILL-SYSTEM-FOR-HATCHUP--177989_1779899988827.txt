==================================================
COMPETITIVE MINI-GAME & SKILL SYSTEM FOR HATCHUP
==================================================

Expand HATCHUP into a highly social and competitive multiplayer experience where players can compete using their Hatchlings in skill-based mini games, races, tournaments, and live events.

Competition should feel:
- fun
- chaotic
- social
- rewarding
- easy to learn
- highly replayable

The system should combine the addictive social competition of:
- Mario Kart
- Fall Guys
- Roblox mini games
- Rocket League
- Pokémon competitions

while remaining simple and child-friendly.

==================================================
CORE COMPETITIVE SYSTEM
==================================================

Players can use their Hatchlings and evolved creatures to compete against other players online in real-time multiplayer mini games.

Competition modes should include:
- flying races
- dragon races
- obstacle courses
- parkour challenges
- hoverboard races
- swimming races
- sky races
- battle arenas
- rhythm dance battles
- puzzle tournaments
- treasure hunts
- team mini games
- survival modes
- capture-the-flag
- dodgeball-style chaos games
- soccer-style mini games
- tag/chase modes

Every creature type should affect gameplay differently.
