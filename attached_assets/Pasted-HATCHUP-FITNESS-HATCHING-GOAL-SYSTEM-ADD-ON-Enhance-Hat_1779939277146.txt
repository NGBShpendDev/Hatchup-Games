HATCHUP FITNESS HATCHING GOAL SYSTEM ADD-ON

Enhance HatchUp Fitness Pals with a balanced real-world fitness progression system where eggs hatch through achievable workout goals that motivate both beginners and experienced fitness users.

The system should feel:
- rewarding
- motivating
- fair
- addictive
- beginner-friendly
- challenging for advanced users

====================================
CORE HATCHING SYSTEM
====================================

Eggs hatch by completing real-life fitness activities.

Base hatch goals should be achievable for beginners while still rewarding dedicated fitness users.

Examples of beginner-friendly hatch goals:
- 5,000 daily steps
- 100 pushups
- 100 squats
- 100 sit-ups or abs exercises
- 20 minutes of cardio
- 30 minutes of walking
- 15 minutes of stretching
- completing a beginner workout class
- drinking daily hydration goals
- completing meditation or recovery sessions

Users can complete:
- one large goal
OR
- combine smaller goals to hatch eggs faster.

Examples:
- 2,500 steps + 50 pushups + hydration goal
- cardio + stretching + meditation
- upper body + lower body + recovery session

====================================
ALL FITNESS STYLES SHOULD COUNT
====================================

Allow all forms of movement and wellness to contribute toward progression.

Supported fitness styles:
- bodybuilding
- walking
- running
- cycling
- yoga
- stretching
- calisthenics
- sports training
- HIIT
- dance fitness
- swimming
- mobility work
- rehab/recovery
- meditation and wellness

The app should never make users feel excluded based on fitness level.

====================================
DIFFICULTY SCALING SYSTEM
====================================

Create adaptive AI scaling.

BEGINNER USERS:
- easier goals
- motivational guidance
- lower barriers to progression
- more frequent rewards
- confidence-building challenges

ADVANCED USERS:
- harder challenges
- elite achievements
- bonus XP multipliers
- rare egg opportunities
- legendary evolutions
- prestige rewards

Examples of advanced hatch goals:
- 15,000 steps
- 500 pushups
- endurance challenges
- marathon-style events
- heavy lifting milestones
- elite streak quests

====================================
SMART AI FITNESS BALANCING
====================================

The AI should:
- analyze user fitness level
- adapt goals dynamically
- prevent burnout
- encourage consistency
- reward effort fairly
- personalize challenges

The system should reward:
- consistency over perfection
- healthy habits
- long-term growth
- effort and participation

====================================

Different workout styles should unlock different progression paths.

Examples:

STRENGTH PATH:

CARDIO PATH:
- running

MIND & RECOVERY PATH:

SPORTS PATH:

====================================
BONUS COMBO SYSTEM
====================================

Reward balanced lifestyles.

Examples:
- workout + hydration bonus

- meditation + stretching combo

Combo bonuses grant:
- bonus XP
- faster egg hatching

- streak boosts

====================================
EMOTIONAL REWARD SYSTEM
====================================

Every completed hatch goal should feel exciting.

Include:
- dramatic hatch animations
- creature celebrations
- level-up effects
- sound effects
- achievement moments
- livestream crowd reactions
- progression fireworks
- social share moments

The goal is to make users emotionally attached to improving their health while making fitness feel like an exciting game instead of a chore.
