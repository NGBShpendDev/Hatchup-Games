GROUP WORKOUT + TEAM ENERGY SYSTEM:

Create a highly social cooperative fitness system where users can group together in real life or virtually to complete workouts, challenges, raids, and events with their HatchUp Pals.

The group system should make fitness feel:
- social
- fun
- motivating
- rewarding
- community-driven
- competitive in a healthy way

WORKOUT PARTY SYSTEM:
Allow users to create or join workout groups called:
- Fitness Parties
- Hatch Crews
- Pal Squads
- Evolution Teams
- Training Crews

Groups can:
- workout together
- complete challenges
- earn team XP
- unlock shared rewards
- battle bosses
- compete on leaderboards

GROUP BONUS XP:
Users earn bonus rewards when exercising with friends.

Examples:
- +10% XP for 2 players
- +25% XP for 4 players
- combo streak multipliers
- shared evolution boosts
- raid energy bonuses
- faster egg hatching

The game should encourage teamwork and accountability.

LIVE GROUP WORKOUTS:
Users can host:
- walking groups
- gym sessions
- running clubs
- cycling events
- fitness classes
- park workouts
- raid meetups

The app should use:
- GPS verification
- proximity detection
- activity syncing

to confirm users are participating together.

TEAM ENERGY SYSTEM:
When groups workout together they generate:
- Team Energy
- Evolution Aura
- Raid Power
- Clan XP

This energy powers:
- special evolutions
- rare events
- giant boss raids
- team unlocks
- community rewards

GROUP CREATURE BONUSES:
Creatures become stronger when training socially.

Examples:
- team workouts unlock fusion boosts
- creatures gain friendship bonds
- coordinated workouts unlock combo evolutions
- squad streaks unlock aura effects

The creatures should visibly react positively during group sessions.

GROUP CHALLENGES:
Create cooperative goals such as:
- 50,000 group steps
- 10 workouts this week
- group hydration goals
- raid victories
- endurance challenges

Completing challenges rewards:
- XP
- cosmetics
- eggs
- titles
- legendary shards
- group badges

LOCAL COMMUNITY SYSTEM:
Allow users to discover nearby:
- workout groups
- raid parties
- walking clubs
- gym crews
- local events

The app should help build real-world fitness communities safely.

GROUP VOICE + CHAT:
Allow:
- team chat
- workout coordination
- raid communication
- encouragement systems
- motivational messages

The communication system should remain:
- positive
- safe
- beginner-friendly
- community-focused

FRIENDSHIP LEVEL SYSTEM:
Users who consistently train together build:
- friendship levels
- synergy bonuses
- shared boosts
- exclusive evolutions

Long-term workout partners unlock:
- special creature interactions
- duo evolutions
- team cosmetics
- friendship auras

This creates emotional attachment and long-term retention.

GROUP RAID BOSSES:
Large groups can challenge giant world bosses together.

Raid bosses should require:
- teamwork
- movement
- coordinated fitness goals
- community participation

Examples:
- global raids
- city raids
- park events
- gym invasions

These events should feel:
- cinematic
- social
- exciting
- viral

SOCIAL MOTIVATION SYSTEM:
Groups should motivate users naturally.

Examples:
- “Your squad is active nearby.”
- “Your team is waiting for you.”
- “Complete today’s squad challenge.”
- “Your Hatch Crew is close to unlocking a legendary egg.”

The game should create healthy accountability without guilt or negativity.

GOAL:
The group system should transform fitness from a lonely activity into:
- a social adventure
- a team experience
- a community lifestyle
- a real-world cooperative game

The app should make users more likely to stay consistent because their friends, teams, and creature pals depend on them.
