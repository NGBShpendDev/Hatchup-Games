HATCHUP FITNESS DIFFICULTY & PROGRESSION CONTROL ADD-ON

Add a customizable progression difficulty system to HatchUp Fitness Pals that allows users to choose their own fitness level and progression speed.

The system should make users feel:
- in control
- motivated
- challenged
- rewarded
- never overwhelmed

Users should be able to change difficulty levels as they improve throughout their fitness journey.

====================================
CHOOSE YOUR FITNESS PATH
====================================

When starting the app, users choose a fitness difficulty level.

Difficulty affects:
- how fast eggs hatch
- XP gain
- workout requirements
- challenge intensity
- evolution speed
- reward rarity

Users can change difficulty levels anytime as they progress.

====================================
DIFFICULTY TIERS
====================================

1. BEGINNER MODE
Perfect for:
- new fitness users
- kids
- recovery users
- casual players

Examples:
- 3,000–5,000 daily steps
- 20–50 pushups
- 20–50 squats
- beginner workouts
- short wellness sessions

Rewards:
- faster early progression
- confidence-building achievements
- more encouragement
- easier streak maintenance

2. INTERMEDIATE MODE
Perfect for:
- consistent gym users
- active people
- moderate athletes

Examples:
- 7,500–10,000 steps
- 100 pushups
- 100 squats
- cardio goals
- strength workouts

Rewards:
- balanced progression
- higher XP
- better egg rarity chances
- stronger evolutions

3. ADVANCED MODE
Perfect for:
- experienced athletes
- hardcore gym users
- competitive players

Examples:
- 15,000+ steps
- 250–500 pushups
- endurance events
- elite workout challenges
- heavy strength training

Rewards:
- bonus XP multipliers
- legendary egg chances
- elite cosmetics
- prestige rankings
- exclusive evolutions

4. LEGEND MODE
Optional ultra-hardcore mode.

Examples:
- marathon challenges
- elite streak goals
- advanced strength targets
- multi-session workouts

Rewards:
- mythic creatures
- global rankings
- ultra rare cosmetics
- legendary titles
- exclusive aura effects

====================================
SMART AI PROGRESSION SYSTEM
====================================

The AI should:
- recommend difficulty levels
- analyze consistency
- prevent burnout
- suggest upgrades when users improve
- suggest lowering difficulty if struggling

The system should always encourage:
- consistency
- healthy progress
- long-term fitness habits

====================================
FLEXIBLE LEVEL SWITCHING
====================================

Users can:
- upgrade difficulty anytime
- downgrade temporarily if injured or busy
- switch based on goals
- experiment with different challenge paths

Examples:
- beginner to intermediate
- strength path to cardio path
- casual mode during vacations
- hardcore mode during transformation challenges

====================================
REWARD BALANCING
====================================

Higher difficulties should provide:
- faster leveling
- rarer rewards
- bonus XP
- prestige recognition
- exclusive evolutions

Lower difficulties should provide:
- more accessible progression
- motivation
- emotional encouragement
- easier streak protection

The system should NEVER shame users for lower difficulty choices.

====================================
EMOTIONAL PROGRESSION DESIGN
====================================

Changing difficulty levels should feel like:
- leveling up in life
- becoming stronger
- entering a new training arc
- evolving as a person

Include:
- cinematic transitions
- new aura effects
- level-up ceremonies
- unlock animations
- mentor-style AI encouragement

The goal is to make every user feel successful regardless of fitness level while still giving elite users deep competitive progression systems.
