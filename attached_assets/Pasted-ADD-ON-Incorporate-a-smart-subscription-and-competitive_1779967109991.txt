Every new user receives:
- full premium access free for 7 days
- access to all features

- battle arena access
- advanced analytics

- all premium fitness tools

The free trial should fully immerse users into the HatchUp universe and make them emotionally attached to progression, streaks, rankings, creatures, and social competition.

After the 7-day free trial:

Users have 3 options:

1. TOP CITY LEADERBOARD EXEMPTION
If a user is ranked in the Top 10 of their city leaderboard:
- they continue receiving premium access for free
- premium status remains active while maintaining Top 10 status
- this creates massive local competition and motivation

If the user drops out of Top 10:
- premium access returns to limited free mode unless subscribed

2. FREE LIMITED VERSION
Users can continue using a free version with limited access.

Free version includes:
- limited HatchUp storage
- limited AI assistant usage
- limited battle entries
- basic progression tools
- basic leaderboards
- limited customization
- ads or reduced rewards
- restricted premium cosmetics
- restricted advanced analytics

The free version should still feel fun and rewarding while encouraging upgrades naturally.

- all AI tools
