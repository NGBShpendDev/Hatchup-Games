HATCHUP POWER PACK & PREMIUM PROGRESSION ADD-ON

Add a powerful monetization and premium progression system to HatchUp Fitness Pals that feels rewarding, fair, motivating, and highly valuable without becoming pay-to-win.

The system should encourage users to:
- stay active
- level up naturally
- unlock premium experiences
- feel excited about progression

====================================
HATCHUP POWER PACK SUBSCRIPTION
====================================

Create a premium subscription called:

“HatchUp Power Pack”

Pricing:
- $8.99 monthly
- $80 yearly subscription

Integrate payments directly with Stripe.

Stripe integration should support:
- subscriptions
- renewals
- upgrades
- cancellations
- promo codes
- free trials
- creator payouts
- secure payments
