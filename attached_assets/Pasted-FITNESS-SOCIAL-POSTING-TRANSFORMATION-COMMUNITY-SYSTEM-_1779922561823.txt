FITNESS SOCIAL POSTING + TRANSFORMATION COMMUNITY SYSTEM:

Create a built-in social posting platform where users can share fitness progress, gym photos, workout moments, transformations, achievements, and HatchUp Pal evolution updates.

The social experience should feel like:
- positive gym culture
- supportive transformation community
- TikTok + Instagram fitness motivation
- creature evolution sharing
- self-improvement celebration

The system should encourage:
- confidence
- motivation
- consistency
- positivity
- community support

NOT toxicity or bullying.

PHOTO + VIDEO POSTING:
Allow users to post:
- gym selfies
- workout clips
- transformation photos
- progress updates
- exercise videos
- running screenshots
- raid meetups
- group workouts
- evolution reveals
- milestone celebrations

Users can pair posts with:
- their creature pals
- evolutions
- streaks
- rankings
- XP gains
- workout stats

Example:
A user posts a gym mirror selfie while their evolved creature appears beside them in AR mode.

AR CREATURE PHOTO SYSTEM:
Allow players to:
- place creatures beside themselves in photos
- pose with their HatchUp Pals
- record evolution videos
- create cinematic transformation clips

This should be optimized for:
- TikTok
- Instagram Reels
- Snapchat
- YouTube Shorts

The goal is to create highly shareable viral content.
