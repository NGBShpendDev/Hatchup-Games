HEALTH APP + SMART DEVICE INTEGRATION:

HatchUp Fitness Pals must deeply integrate with the native health and fitness systems already installed on users’ devices.

The app should automatically sync and track activity using:

IOS:
- Apple Health
- Apple Watch
- Fitness App
- HealthKit APIs

ANDROID:
- Google Fit
- Samsung Health
- Fitbit integration
- Health Connect APIs

WEARABLE SUPPORT:
Support:
- Apple Watch
- Fitbit
- Garmin
- Samsung Galaxy Watch
- WHOOP
- Oura Ring
- smart fitness devices

The app should automatically import:
- steps
- walking distance
- running
- calories burned
- heart rate
- workouts
- sleep
- hydration
- active minutes
- exercise sessions

The syncing experience should feel:
- automatic
- smooth
- accurate
- seamless
- battery efficient

PASSIVE PROGRESSION:
Users should continue earning:
- XP
- hatch progress
- evolution energy
- coins
- streaks

even when the app is closed through health app syncing.

This is extremely important for retention and daily engagement.

BACKGROUND TRACKING:
Allow background syncing so users feel constantly rewarded for real-life movement.

Examples:
- walking at work progresses eggs
- gym workouts evolve creatures
- sleep improves recovery stats
- hydration boosts aura progression

The game should feel connected to the player’s actual lifestyle.

FITNESS DETECTION:
Automatically detect workout types such as:
- running
- walking
- cycling
- strength training
- HIIT
- yoga
- stretching
- sports activity

Different activities should affect different creature stats and evolutions.

SMART HEALTH AI:
Use health data to create personalized:
- workout suggestions
- meal plans
- recovery recommendations
- hydration reminders
- streak encouragement
- sleep improvement tips

The AI coach should adapt based on real user activity.

PRIVACY + TRUST:
Health data handling must feel:
- safe
- transparent
- secure
- user-controlled

Include:
- privacy settings
- permission management
- encrypted data storage
- clear explanations of tracked activity

Users should feel comfortable syncing their health information.

OFFLINE PROGRESSION:
Even if users forget to open the app, their movement should still count toward:
- creature growth
- XP progression
- egg hatching
- streak systems
- realm progression

This creates a constant feeling of progression tied to real life.

GOAL:
The app should seamlessly transform everyday movement into meaningful in-game progression without requiring users to constantly open the app manually.
