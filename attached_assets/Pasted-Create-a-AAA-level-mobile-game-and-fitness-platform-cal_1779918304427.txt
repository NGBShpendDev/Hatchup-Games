Create a AAA-level mobile game and fitness platform called “HatchUp.”

HatchUp is a revolutionary mobile game inspired by Pokémon GO, RPG creature evolution games, social competition, fitness tracking, and real-world exploration.

The goal is to make fitness addictive, emotional, social, and rewarding through creature hatching, evolution, progression systems, and real-world movement.

This should feel like:
- Pokémon GO meets fitness
- Duolingo streak addiction
- Clash Royale progression systems
- Tamagotchi emotional attachment
- Social MMO elements
- RPG creature collection
- TikTok-style virality

CORE PHILOSOPHY:
Users should NOT feel like they are using a fitness app.
Users should feel like they are playing a fun, addictive creature evolution game that secretly improves their health, confidence, discipline, and lifestyle.

CORE GAME LOOP:
1. Move in real life
2. Gain fitness XP
3. Hatch eggs
4. Evolve creatures
5. Unlock powers/cosmetics/worlds
6. Battle and socialize
7. Compete in events
8. Continue progression daily

Every real-world action contributes XP and creature progression.

CREATURE SYSTEM:
Users hatch creatures from eggs.

Every creature has:
- Genetics
- Traits
- Personality
- Rarity
- Evolution trees
- Elements
- RPG stats

Creature classes include:
- Titans (strength)
- Sprinters (cardio)
- Mystics (recovery/wellness)
- Beasts (hardcore training)
- Hybrids (balanced builds)
- Legendary hidden evolutions

No two creatures should evolve exactly the same.
