Create a AAA-quality mobile game and social fitness platform called “HatchUp Fitness.”

SLOGAN:
“Every Step Evolves You.”

MISSION:
HatchUp Fitness transforms real-life movement, workouts, wellness, and self-improvement into an addictive fantasy RPG adventure where users hatch, evolve, collect, and battle creatures through real-world fitness progression.

The app should feel like:
- Pokémon GO meets fitness
- Duolingo habit addiction
- Clash Royale polish
- Tamagotchi emotional attachment
- MMORPG progression systems
- TikTok-style social virality

CORE PHILOSOPHY:
This should NOT feel like a fitness app.
This should feel like an addictive creature evolution game that secretly improves users’ health, confidence, discipline, consistency, and lifestyle.
